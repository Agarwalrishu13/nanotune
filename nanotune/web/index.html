The songs

How this works

1. Open a folder. Any folder with music in it — songs in sub-folders are found too. It is remembered for next time.

2. Press a song. It plays right on the page. ⏭ plays the next one, 🔀 mixes them up, 🔁 repeats them all.

3. That is the whole thing. No account, no upload, no “sign in to continue listening”. The songs stay exactly where they are.

A song that will not play? The browser plays mp3, wav, ogg, m4a, flac and a few others. Anything else is listed honestly as unplayable, not hidden.
